GLR Classlists (DB)

Term → School → Programme → Year classlists built from argos.db module_class_list runs. Contact fields removed.

DB-backed
How to use
  • Select a Term, then a School, then a Programme, then a Year of Study.
  • Summary counts update when a programme is selected. The class list appears after picking a year.
  • Columns shown: STUDENT_ID, LAST_NAME, FIRST_NAME, REGISTRATION_STATUS. Email/phone are intentionally omitted.
  • Year is taken from a numeric classlist value when present; non-numeric classlist values fall back to GLR numeric year, otherwise blank.
  • Summary includes GLR-only students (not present on classlists) for the selected filters.
  • Term coverage (argos.db module_class_list): 202510. Additional terms will appear after new ingests.
Filters
Available terms from module_class_list runs: 202510. Only these appear in the filter; new ingests will add further terms.
Contact fields are not included in this DB-backed build.
