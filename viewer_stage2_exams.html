Notes
  • Rows are derived from the January Akari vs Banner selection (one per School/Programme/Year).
  • Programme = Stage 2 code; CrosswalkedProgramme = resolver (programme_id_resolver) canonical code (e.g., TU826 → TU805).
  • Students/StudentsCrosswalked come from GLR term 202510 (unique learners per Programme/Year and CrosswalkedProgramme/Year); 0 means no GLR count.
  • AkariStatus reflects the Akari accuracy flags: AkariOK or AkariCheckRequired.
  • Year hints: blank years use Akari module years only; programmes with no Akari years are omitted.
  • Dedup: unique per School + Programme + CrosswalkedProgramme + Year to show aliases without duplicates.
  • Categories:
    • A: January start (City); mid-stage; operate under legacy assessment regulations in legacy Banner.
    • B: January start (Tallaght/Blanchardstown); mid-stage; operate under legacy assessment regs in legacy Banner or outside new Banner.
    • C: Postgraduate requiring January module/award boards for Spring graduation; exit boards on usual timeline; repeat-only generally excluded unless requested by School.
    • D: Undergraduate requiring January module/award boards for Spring graduation; repeat-only generally excluded unless requested by School.
    • F: Minor awards needing module/award boards asap; not constrained by Spring graduation date.
    • G: Programme boards only for repeating students; typically defer to June unless recategorised (e.g., CORU needs).
