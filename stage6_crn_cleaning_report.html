Reason codes:
These codes show which rule-based approach was used to determine the final Proposed CRN to use.
single_crn_to_use — only one CRN provided in CRN2Use.
multi_crn_to_use_by_brightspace — multiple CRNs in CRN2Use; chose the one with the most Brightspace students (tie→Banner, in-row, numeric).
fallback_banner_crn — no CRN2Use values; chose from Banner CRNs by most Brightspace students (tie→Banner, numeric).
no_crn_available — no CRNs found in Banner or CRN2Use.
The F column applies the same ranking but excludes CRNs that also appear in Banner term 202510; if CRN2Use values are all excluded, it falls back to Banner CRNs (blank means no eligible CRN).
